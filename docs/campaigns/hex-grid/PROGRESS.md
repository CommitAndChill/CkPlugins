### ActorRelay.Generic save inspection

**The packaged duplicate-key inspection blocker is closed for newly written saves.** ActorRelay channels intentionally share a group key so descendants of multiple saved channels can reconnect to one live channel. Each channel still needs a distinct saved row for owner references. The old inspector had no serialized record of the sharing policy and reported the identical GUID as an error. All three pooled round-trip tests reproduced the failure before the fix. Pre-fix gate (source workstation, `actorrelay-key-red.log`)

CkSnapshot now records a tagged header entry containing the saved ID and group GUID for every member of a complete, explicitly shared group. The inspector reconciles the claims against the entity and payload tables. A valid group has distinct, unambiguous engine-owned rows with matching keys and no member-owned payloads. Ordinary and mixed groups retain the duplicate-key error. Incomplete, mismatched, repeated or dangling claims are errors. The loader, pooling policy and native v8 table layout are unchanged. Older unmarked saves remain loadable and still receive the original diagnostic when inspected. The lowest-risk path keeps world replacement and the readiness contract, profiles empty-world travel and tests improvements first on the zero-object control, then on both full manifests.
